# Translation glossary

Every translation follows this table: one term, one translation, everywhere. A translator or checker who wants to change a term changes it here first, then in every entry that uses it.

## Style

| | Khmer | Chinese (Simplified) |
|---|---|---|
| Addressing the reader | អ្នក, plain and polite; no royal or very formal words | 你 (friends' app, not 您) |
| Buttons | a short verb phrase, as in Khmer banking apps (រក្សាទុក, បោះបង់) | a short verb (保存, 取消) |
| Digits, money, dates | Western digits (1,250.00); currency codes stay (USD, KHR) | same |
| Brand and product names | Share Expense, Google, ExchangeRate-API, AI stay in Latin letters | same |
